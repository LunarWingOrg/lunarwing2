### Other Info

The Enlightenment (18th-century European movement)

    啓蒙 (けいもう, keimō) — the standard term, e.g. 啓蒙時代 "the Age of Enlightenment", 啓蒙主義 "Enlightenment (as an ideology)"
    啓蒙思想 (keimō shisō) — Enlightenment thought/philosophy

Meiji Japan's own "enlightenment" era

    文明開化 (ぶんめいかいか, bunmei kaika) — "civilization and enlightenment," the Meiji-era modernization catchphrase; this is the closest native parallel to a cultural "enlightenment" as a movement

The Renaissance

    文芸復興 (ぶんげいふっこう, bungei fukkō) — the kanji term, literally "revival of arts and letters"
    ルネサンス (runesansu) — the katakana loanword, actually more common in everyday usage

If you're naming something (project, server, whatever), 啓蒙 or 文芸復興 carry the historical weight; 悟り has the Zen connotation.
