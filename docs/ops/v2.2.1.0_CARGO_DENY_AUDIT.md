## Outcome

- **No unaccepted, reachable vulnerability, no unsound finding outside the
  accepted set, and no yanked registry pin was found.** The only unaccepted
  raw-lock vulnerability IDs are the two `pyo3` advisories whose package never
  resolves into a compiled build graph.
- All four advisory gates pass against the current RustSec database with the
  CI-pinned binaries. An independent `cargo-audit` pass completed over all 27
  lockfiles, and a direct sparse-index pass checked every registry pin and
  checksum live.
- **All 14 acceptances in `ic/deny.toml` are still necessary and still match
  their locked copies.** None is stale, removable, or widen-able. No ignore was
  added, removed, widened, or re-worded by this item.
- **One ownership finding, new to this audit: the clearing owner named by 12
  of the 14 acceptances is closed.** WI#367 (four `libsignal-protocol`-chain
  entries) and WI#418 (eight libSQL-chain entries) were both closed on
  2026-10-18 without clearing, and the owner-liveness half of the WI#363 guard
  was dropped, so no gate notices. The acceptances themselves remain valid;
  only the owner reference is stale. Reported here and filed as **WI#779**;
  not fixed here because re-pointing an owner is a policy change. See
  [Orphaned clearing owners](#orphaned-clearing-owners-wi367-and-wi418).
- **No advisory in this database revision matches the graph unaccepted.**
  The six newest advisories (2026-11-17) are all against `lettre`, which is
  absent from all 27 locks and from every manifest.
- **No dependency, manifest, lockfile, vendored source, or CI gate changed.**
  The audit's only tree change is this report.

  -2021-0145) closed on **2026-10-18T06:22:49Z**. `failure 0.1.8`,

  `rustls 0.22.4`, `rustls-webpki 0.102.8` and `h2 0.3.27` are still locked.
  This item had been explicitly re-opened on 2026-10-17 (note 9872) because
  "a closed work item cannot own a standing acceptance", and its decision
  record still says "the **open work item WI#418** is their clearing owner
  until the entries are removed". It was closed again about seven hours later.
- **Nothing fails on this.** The WI#363 guard that was meant to catch a closed
  named owner was scope-reduced: `.gitlab/tests/test_deny_toml_guards.py`
  states "The online owner-liveness half of MR !1795 was dropped by the scope
  decision: no owner-liveness job or network call exists." The four gates and
  all five guard suites pass with the closed owners in place, which is exactly
  the false-green this audit is meant to surface.

  decision itself (2026-11-17) with "no migration planned". That is the

- **Filed as [WI#779](https://git.lunarwing.org/lunarwing/lunarwing2/-/work_items/779)**:
