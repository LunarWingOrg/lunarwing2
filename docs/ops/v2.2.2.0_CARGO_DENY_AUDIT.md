### The `[bans]` wrapper pin: what it does and does not do

The `heapless` entry is `{ name = "heapless", version = "<0.9.4", wrappers =
["postcard"] }`. Stated precisely, per the v2.2.1.0 review's AHY-1:

- It **does** require that every crate which depends directly on `heapless`
  be listed in `wrappers`. A new direct dependent that is not `postcard`
  fails the bans gate.
- It **does not** prevent a new crate from reaching `heapless` transitively
  through `postcard`, nor prevent `postcard` from being reached through a new
  parent. The nine ID-keyed ignores still match whatever path arrives.
- What actually keeps the acceptance narrow is the *reachability* judgement in
  the table above plus the fact that the pinned copy is 0.7.17; the wrapper
  list is a tripwire for a new direct edge, not a containment boundary.
