## #317 (OPEN) — Boot-time restart recorder saves mission snapshots without the mission lock — can overwrite a concurrent fire (lost update)

**Author:** cmc | **Created:** 2026-10-05 12:17Z | **Labels:** 2.1.6.0, bug, engine, engine-v2, low, mission, reliability

P-6 from the !1431 review (note #6231), pre-existing, byte-identical in
tenant build #2. `MissionManager::record_restart_interrupted_missions`
iterates `list_all_missions_global()` snapshots and saves them back without
taking `mission_lock` or reloading (`mission.rs:2502-2520`, item's anchors
source-verified at `7bfe524e9`), while every other writer (`fire_mission_inner`,
the outcome watcher) serializes on that lock. Boot order arms the concurrent
writers first — resumed-thread watchers, re-armed retry wakes (1 s fallback
for overdue), then the recorder (`ic/src/bridge/router.rs:1764` re-verified
at HEAD); the 30 s-warmup cron ticker is not part of the race. Consequence: a
silent lost update — dropped `thread_history` entry, reverted
`last_fired_at`, undercounted `threads_today`, dropped trigger dedup key.
