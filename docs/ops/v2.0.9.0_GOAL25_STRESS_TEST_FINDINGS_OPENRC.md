## Additional findings from the fresh tenant

### F6 — mt-admin falsely reports a live OpenRC WeeChat service as `unknown`

Severity: medium operator-observability defect.

Every `mt-admin status stress25r2` call printed:

```text
lunarwing-weechat-stress25r2: unknown
lunarwing-weechat-adapter-stress25r2: started
```

The WeeChat service was demonstrably live: 105 focused/soak messages traversed
it, the relay answered, and teardown stopped it successfully. Root cause is a
text-contract mismatch in current source: the custom WeeChat OpenRC `status()`
prints `session is up` / `session is not running`
(`ic/scripts/mt-admin-lib/08-units-openrc.sh:318-329`), while pre-fix mt-admin
status extracted only `started|stopped|crashed`.

Resolved 2026-08-28 (`3dcfa273`, `05e78793`):
`openrc_service_state` now preserves standard OpenRC states, treats a successful
custom `status()` return as `started`, maps WeeChat's explicit not-running result
to `stopped`, and reserves `unknown` for an unrecognized failure
(`ic/scripts/mt-admin-lib/04-tenant-lifecycle.sh:1253-1270,1401-1404`). The new
`ic/scripts/tests/test-mt-admin-openrc-status.sh` regression covers standard and
custom states plus full tenant-status output, asserting that a healthy custom
WeeChat service is reported as `started` and never `unknown`.

### F7 — fresh-tenant channel health is green while optional channels fail

Severity: medium monitoring/configuration defect; not a stress data-path failure.

The fresh tenant reported HTTP 200 and `healthy:true` for every channel in
`/api/gateway/status`, including `multica`, `darkirc`, and `xmpp`. During the same
run:

- multica failed startup on every daemon start (5 times) due null/unconfigured
  fields;
- the supposedly disabled DarkIRC integration made 328 failed poll calls and 15
  failed health calls to unused loopback port 6680;
- the unconfigured XMPP bridge logged 180 connection failures.

The green status is explainable in current code, but misleading:

- gateway status directly presents `ChannelManager::health_check_all`
  (`ic/src/channels/web/server.rs:2936-2955`);
- WASM channel health checks sender/poll-task liveness and poll staleness, not
  callback success (`ic/src/channels/wasm/wrapper.rs:2933-2974`);
- native XMPP health is unconditional `Ok(())`
  (`ic/src/channels/xmpp/mod.rs:1286-1288`).

Recommendation: do not activate optional channels until configured/enabled, and
propagate sustained callback/connection failures into channel health. At
minimum, a failed channel `start` must not later appear healthy solely because
its wrapper task is alive.

### F8 — generated OpenRC service uses deprecated `--chuid`

Severity: low/future compatibility.

Every restart/teardown emitted:

```text
WARNING: -c/--chuid is deprecated and will be removed in the future, please use -u/--user instead
```

The generated WeeChat unit invokes `start-stop-daemon --chuid` for tenant-scoped
tmux operations (`ic/scripts/mt-admin-lib/08-units-openrc.sh:302-305,341-348`).
The replacement must preserve actual privilege dropping and access to the
tenant-owned tmux socket; update with an OpenRC integration test rather than a
blind flag substitution.
