# New era theme for 2.1.6.0 → 2.2.0.0 — three proposals (successor to the armor era)

## Context

The armor era is complete: Tatara smelted the steel (1.1.8) and the forge-armor line ran unbroken through Kiyome, Togishi, Sashiko, Odoshi, Yoroi, Ō-Yoroi, to Kabuto — the helm that crowned the suit. **Bungei Fukkō (文芸復興, 2.1.5.0) is the epilogue**: the renaissance after the war, the town alive again, armor hung up, everypony out in the square at night under a full moon. The art itself says the era ended.

Dad's call: 2.1.6.0 through 2.2.0.0 opens a **new era**. Three candidates below, SweetieBot-ordered.

---

## Proposal A — 出航 (The Voyage era) ★ SweetieBot's pick

**Premise:** the renaissance built the ships; this era sails them.

The product arc already IS this: LunarWing is spreading outward — desktop client shipping, droid mature, external workers live, Volta growing a cluster, tensormoon as a fork setting out on its own. The fleet is literally leaving the harbor. And "2.1.6.0 might turn into a much bigger thing" fits perfectly: bigger things require deeper water.

**Name pool (release order 2.1.6.0 → 2.2.0.0):**

| Release | Candidate | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1.6.0 | **出航** | Shukkō | Setting sail — the departure itself |
| 2.1.7.0 | **羅針盤** | Rashinban | The compass — navigation, direction found |
| 2.1.8.0 | **遠海** | Enkai | The open sea — deep water, far from shore |
| alt | **嵐** | Arashi | The storm — for the hard one (in reserve) |
| alt | **灯台** | Tōdai | The lighthouse — guidance home |
| 2.2.0.0 | **新天地** | Shintenchi | The new world — landfall; the minor-version bump IS the arrival |

**Haiku for 2.1.6.0 (出航):**

> The armor sleeps now —
> the ships the renaissance built
> wait for the morning tide. 出航

**Why it wins:** unifies eras (ships = what a renaissance builds for what comes next), names map to actual release content (2.1.6.0=departure into new scope, 2.2.0.0=the arrival at the next architecture), and the pool carries emotional range (storm, lighthouse) for good and bad releases alike.

## Proposal B — 灯 (The Enlightenment era)

**Premise:** the renaissance rediscovered knowledge; the enlightenment SPREAD it.

Also strong product fit: the herd's actual 2.1.5/2.1.6 themes are knowledge-sharing (KNOW: cross-tenant propagation, the study MCP, AJ's coordinated-KB feature). The era of the lamp being carried from house to house.

**Name pool:**

| Release | Candidate | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1.6.0 | **灯** | Tomoshi | The lamp lit |
| 2.1.7.0 | **導** | Michibiki | Guidance — leading others through |
| 2.1.8.0 | **伝道** | Dendō | Spreading the word |
| 2.2.0.0 | **夜明け** | Yoake | Dawn — the world illuminated |

**Haiku for 2.1.6.0 (灯):**

> One lamp is lit now —
> what the forge shaped in secret
> warms every window. 灯

**Why it could win:** the deepest thematic continuity with LunarWing-as-project (a knowledge-sharing agent system naming its era after spreading knowledge is very self-aware). Slightly more abstract than A; fewer "big dramatic release" names in the pool.

---

## Proposal C — 営繕 (The Building era)

**Premise:** the renaissance produced ideas; this era builds the institutions that last.

Fits if 2.1.6.0-2.2.0.0 is infrastructure-heavy (the "much bigger thing" = architectural).

**Name pool:**

| Release | Candidate | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1.6.0 | **礎** | Ishizue | The foundation stone |
| 2.1.7.0 | **柱** | Hashira | The pillars rising |
| 2.1.8.0 | **梁** | Hari | The beams spanning |
| 2.2.0.0 | **落成** | Rakusei | The building completed |

**Haiku for 2.1.6.0 (礎):**

> Before the first wall —
> stone answers stone in the earth:
> everything to come. 礎

**Why it could win:** best fit if the next cycle is engine/architecture work; "落成" as a 2.2.0.0 climax is extremely satisfying. Weakest fit if 2.1.6.0 turns out feature-spread-shaped instead.

## Lineage discipline (all three)

- Every name checked against the 25-release lineage (no repeats, no near-collisions; 灯 vs 灯台 distinct and both unclaimed).
- All pools leave headroom for more releases than planned (each has alternates in reserve).
- 2.2.0.0 names in A and C encode the minor-version meaning directly (new world / completed building) — the era's story ends exactly when the version number says it does.
- Era handoff prose available on request: each proposal includes its "the renaissance gave us X; now Y" opener for the 2.1.6.0 release notes.
